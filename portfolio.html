Web app

Economic Development Map

A web application designed to visualize economic development initiatives across the City.

Economic Development approached IT with a need to visualize the City's economic development initiatives in a web application. I designed and built an ArcGIS Experience Builder application that allows users to explore the City's initiatives (tax zones, development districts) and view related details. The app is used by both the public and internal staff to track progress and inform decision-making.

Dashboard and Web app

Visitor Center Digital Guestbook Solution

A web app pipeline for managing and visualizing guestbook entries at the Visitor Center.

After the City's Visitor Center ruled out costly commercial guestbook software, I utilized existing City GIS tools and infrastructure to develop a working alternative. A dashboard visualizes points as visitors add them on the main lobby TV, and the Experience Builder app powered by Survey123 allows guests to input their information and view the map freely. (Internal use only, no live link available.)

Role
GIS Technician Intern
Timeline
2025
Data
City of College Station
Outcome
An impactful alternative to expensive software that allows the visitor center to have an interactive element with guests and digitally manage/export guest information.
  • ArcGIS Online
  • Dashboards
  • Experience Builder
  • Survey123
